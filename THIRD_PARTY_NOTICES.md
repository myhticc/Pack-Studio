# Third-party notices

Pack Studio is an unofficial Minecraft Java Edition resource pack editor. It is not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.

## Artwork

The offline library contains artwork from Shade and CC0 equipment collections curated by AntumDeluge, including work by 7Soul1 / Henrique Lazarini, Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup contributors, ETTiNGRiNDER and ScratchIO. Additional pixel artwork and armor templates were created for Pack Studio.

Each entry in `ui/library.json` records its source, author and license. Collection download sources are recorded in `ui/credits.json`, and attribution files from the source archives are retained under `ui/library/licenses`. These artwork entries are distributed under CC0-1.0. Used artwork provenance is included in exported resource packs.
